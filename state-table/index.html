Water-system results by state

Each water system is counted once. The table includes active community water systems with complete monitoring and does not rank water quality or personal health risk.

“Percent at or above a frozen cutoff” is the share of active community water systems with complete monitoring that had at least one complete-set sampling location with a yearly average at or above an EPA January 2026 technical-assistance cutoff. It is not a compliance determination and does not mean PFAS was absent from other systems.
