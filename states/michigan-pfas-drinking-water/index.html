How the count works

What is included

Each active community water system with complete monitoring is counted once, even if it is linked with several ZIP codes. Private wells are excluded, and UCMR 5 did not comprehensively monitor every small system. This page applies the dated federal comparison levels used in the research, not Michigan drinking-water standards. UCMR 5 results alone do not determine compliance with either framework.
