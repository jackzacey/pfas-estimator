Interpretation boundaries

What these results mean

The denominator is active community water systems with complete UCMR 5 monitoring in this state or territory. The numerator is unique systems with at least one complete-set sampling location meeting an individual PFAS or qualifying Hazard Index technical comparison. Systems are never copied or counted once per ZIP. UCMR 5 results do not establish household service, individual exposure, health risk, or regulatory compliance. Private wells are excluded, and small systems were not comprehensively monitored.
