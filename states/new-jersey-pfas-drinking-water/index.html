What these results mean

The percentage compares active community water systems with complete monitoring. A system is counted when at least one complete-set sampling location met an individual PFAS or Hazard Index technical comparison. Each system is counted once, even if it is linked with several ZIP codes. These results do not identify a home’s utility, exposure, health risk, or compliance. Private wells are excluded, and small systems were not comprehensively monitored.
