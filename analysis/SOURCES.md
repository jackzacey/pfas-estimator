# Source register

This register distinguishes authoritative data/rule sources from peer-reviewed scientific literature. Each manuscript claim will later be mapped to a source in an evidence matrix.

## Authoritative data and rules

1. EPA UCMR 5 occurrence files and January 2026 Data Summary: <https://www.epa.gov/dwucmr/fifth-unregulated-contaminant-monitoring-rule-data-finder>
2. EPA. Selection of Nationally Representative Public Water Systems for the Unregulated Contaminant Monitoring Rule: 2021 Update. EPA 815-B-21-012: <https://www.regulations.gov/document/EPA-HQ-OW-2020-0530-0127>
3. EPA April 2024 PFAS NPDWR and Hazard Index guidance: <https://www.epa.gov/dwreginfo/pfas-rule-implementation>
4. EPA May 2026 proposed rescission of PFHxS, PFNA, HFPO-DA, and Hazard Index provisions: <https://www.epa.gov/sdwa/proposed-pfas-rescission-rule>
5. EPA Public Water System Service Areas version 3, March 2026: <https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/public-water-system-service-areas>
6. EPA SDWIS Federal Reporting Services: <https://sdwis.epa.gov/ords/sfdw_pub/r/sfdw/sdwis_fed_reports_public/1?p1_report=WD>
7. US Census 2020–2024 ACS 5-year estimates: <https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.html>

## Search update rule

Search PubMed plus Scopus or Web of Science, perform backward and forward citation chaining, and repeat the 2025–present search within 7 days of submission. Log complete search strings, dates, inclusion decisions, DOI/PMID, and the exact manuscript claim supported.
