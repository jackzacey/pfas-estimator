EPA UCMR 5 Data (2023–2025)
This report lists public water systems linked to the entered ZIP. It summarizes a fixed research snapshot of EPA results received through January 15, 2026. Each system card shows when EPA collected its samples. A result below an EPA reporting level does not mean the concentration was zero or that the compound was absent.

This report cannot identify the water system for a specific home or determine current legal compliance, household tap-water levels, personal exposure, or health risk.
About the project

About this independent research

PFAS Estimator helps people find and read EPA public-water monitoring data. It was built by Jack Zhang in Los Gatos, California. Joshua Tamayo-Sarver, MD, PhD, provided early feedback on clinical framing and limitations.

The archived data and code are public, and automated internal checks are in place. The analysis has not yet been independently reproduced or peer reviewed.
