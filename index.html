Preliminary research snapshot

System-level environmental justice analysis

The paper analysis is independent of the interface but uses the same frozen release. These findings are preliminary and have not completed journal peer review.

8,069 census-monitored community water systems in the primary inferential cohort
964 systems meeting at least one April 2024 technical comparison criterion
11.9% primary-cohort system prevalence (964 of 8,069)
1.15 adjusted prevalence ratio per 10-point increase in Hispanic service-area composition (95% CI, 1.09–1.22)

Primary design

  • Community water systems serving at least 3,300 people, the UCMR 5 census-monitored size range.
  • EPA sampling-location annual averages and Hazard Index comparisons, not ZIP maxima.
  • EPA service-area-to-block-group crosswalk with residential-building weights.
  • Modified-Poisson prevalence ratios with region adjustment and spatially robust uncertainty.

Results that must not be oversold

  • Black and American Indian/Alaska Native composition estimates were null or imprecise.
  • Poverty and rural composition were inversely associated with the benchmark outcome.
  • Associations are ecological and cross-sectional; they are not individual exposure or causal effects.
  • The January 2026 UCMR 5 release is not final; EPA plans a final data release in fall 2026.

Interpretation

Read system results as monitoring evidence—not a personal risk score

An “above comparison” flag means at least one complete-set sampling location met EPA’s unrounded technical-assistance classification for an annual average or Hazard Index. UCMR 5 results do not by themselves establish compliance or noncompliance with the drinking-water rule.

ZIP associations come from EPA’s PWS service-ZIP file. Water-system boundaries and ZIP codes can overlap imperfectly, and several systems may be associated with one ZIP. Contact the named utility to verify whether it serves your address and to obtain its current consumer confidence report.

This site does not provide medical advice. For evidence-based clinical guidance about known PFAS exposure, see the National Academies guidance.

Data source: EPA UCMR 5 results received through January 15, 2026. Comparisons reproduce EPA technical-assistance classifications and are not compliance determinations. The research analysis is preliminary and not yet peer reviewed.
