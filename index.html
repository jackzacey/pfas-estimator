Why these results matter

EPA monitoring can show whether PFAS were measured at a public water system’s sampling locations and whether an annual average met a technical comparison. That information can help communities ask more specific questions of their utilities.

The lookup cannot determine which utility serves a particular home or estimate what any person drank. Use a water bill or utility record to confirm your provider, then consult that utility’s current water-quality information.

The system-level research found that 964 of 8,069 primary-cohort community water systems (11.9%) met at least one April 2024 technical comparison. The analysis is ecological, cross-sectional, preliminary, and not yet peer reviewed.
