About the project

Independent PFAS monitoring research

PFAS Estimator makes EPA public-water monitoring data easier to find and understand. It was built by Jack Zhang in Los Gatos, California. Joshua Tamayo-Sarver, MD, PhD, provided early feedback on clinical framing and limitations.
