About PFAS Estimator

Built by Jack Zhang to make public drinking-water results easier to find and understand. Joshua Tamayo-Sarver, MD, PhD, provided early feedback on clinical framing and limitations.

Justin Ting supported marketing and outreach for PFAS Estimator.
