Clear next steps

Use the result to make a practical water plan

1. Confirm your water system. Look for the utility name on your water bill. A ZIP code can include several systems, so this is the most important first step.

2. Read the monitoring comparison. The site shows the highest yearly average reported across each system’s EPA sampling locations. “At or above” and “below” describe the frozen technical-assistance cutoff only. They do not determine legal compliance, a household tap concentration, personal exposure, or health risk.

3. Check current official information. Read the utility’s current Consumer Confidence Report or contact the utility about newer sampling, treatment, and compliance information. If you independently choose to consider a filter, verify an exact NSF/ANSI 53 or NSF/ANSI 58 PFAS-reduction claim and follow the replacement schedule.
