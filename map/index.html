Interpretation

Study comparisons reproduce EPA’s technical classifications based on the April 2024 federal levels. They do not establish current compliance, household exposure, or health risk. State summaries require a complete yearly average at one or more sampling locations.

Coverage

UCMR 5 did not comprehensively monitor every small system. Private wells are excluded. Missing monitoring or map locations do not establish that PFAS are absent.
